Bihar Board Class 10th, 12th Compartmental Result 2026 Notice

Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) may release matric and intermediate compartmental and special examination 2026 result in the month of May 2026. The compartmental and special examination was held from 02 May 2026 to 11 May 2026 across the state as per the notified examination centers by the board. All appeared candidates may download their results online through the board official websites i.e. https://biharboardonline.com with Roll Code and Roll Number only.

How To Download Compartmental Exam Result 2026

Follow these steps to download class 10th and 12th compartmental and special examination 2026 result:

  1. Visit the Bihar Board official website i.e. https://biharboardonline.com
  2. On the home page under important links section click on the provided link 'Click Here For Matric / Intermediate compartmental and special exam Result 2026'
  3. You will be redirected to the Class 10th and 12th compartmental and special examination 2026 result download page.
  4. In the result download page enter your Roll Code and Roll Number correctly in the specified input field.
  5. Next solve mathematical Captcha and fill in correct value in the given field.
  6. Finally click on the View button to download your compartmental and special exam result from Board server.
