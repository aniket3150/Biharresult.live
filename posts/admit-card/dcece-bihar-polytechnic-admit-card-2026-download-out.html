Bihar Polytechnic Admit Card 2026 Instructions

a. Following the scrutiny of the received applications, admit cards for the examination may be downloaded from the Board's website bceceboard.bihar.gov.in by those applicants deemed eligible, these admit cards shall be valid for appearing in the examination. Upon obtaining the admit card, every applicant is required to carefully read the instructions inscribed thereon.

b. It is mandatory for every examinee to thoroughly verify all information and entries printed on their Admit Card immediately upon receipt. Should any discrepancy be found, the examinee must contact the Controller of Examinations. This must be done in person on any working day, between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM, no later than five days prior to the date of the examination. Failure to do so will result in no further corrections being entertained after this deadline, and the examinee shall be held solely responsible for any errors present on their Admit Card.

c. Candidates are required to affix their full signature on the admit card at the time of the examination. It is also mandatory for the candidate's signature to appear on the examination center's copy in every shift, in the presence of the invigilator.

How To Download DCECE PE & Paramedical Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) official website https://bceceboard.bihar.gov.in
  2. On the home page under Download Section click on the provided link 'Download DCECE 2026 Admit Card'.
  3. You will be redirected to the admit card download page of Bihar Polytechnic (Engineering and Paramedical) Entrance Exam 2026.
  4. In the admit card download page under DCECE 2026 section enter your Application Number and DOB (dd-mm-yyyy) correctly in the specified input field.
  5. Finally click on Submit button to download your admit card from BCECE Board server.
