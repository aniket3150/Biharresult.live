Bihar Board Class 10th / 12th 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027

Post Date: 26 November 2026, 2:12 PM

The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) has released the second dummy admit card for the Class 10th and Class 12th Annual Examination 2027. Students can use the official links below to view their card and carefully check the details printed on it.

2nd Dummy Admit Card Available
