Indian Army Agniveer Exam Scheme 2026

Online test will be objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ). Agniveer General Duty, Technician and Tradesmen candidates will answer 50 questions in one hour and Agniveer Clerk / Store Keeper Technical will answer 100 questions in two hours.

Marking Pattern:

  1. Full marks for every correct answer.
  2. Nil marks for unattempted questions.
  3. Attempted questions marked for review will also be evaluated.

How To Download Agniveer Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit official website: https://joinindianarmy.nic.in
  2. Enter captcha and click Enter Website.
  3. Open link: ADMIT CARD FOR COMMON ENTRANCE EXAM FOR AGNIVEER, JCOs / OR FOR THE YR 2026-27.
  4. On candidate login page enter JIA Roll Number and User Password.
  5. Enter captcha and click Login to download admit card.
