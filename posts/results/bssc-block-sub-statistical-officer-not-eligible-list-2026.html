BSSC Block / Sub Statistical Officer Not Eligible List 2026

Updated On: 15 May 2026, 10:12 PM

Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) invites online applications for recruitment to the post of Sub Statistical Officer (SSO) / Block Statistical Officer (BSO) for 682 vacancies under Directorate of Economics and Statistics Department of Planning and Development for Advertisement No. 01/2025.

All eligible and interested candidates can apply online at BSSC official website bssc.bihar.gov.in or onlinebssc.com from 01st April 2025 to till the application last date 21st April 2025 for BSO / SSO recruitment in BSSC.
