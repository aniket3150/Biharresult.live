Bihar Vidhan Sabha Recruitment 2026: 20 Junior Secretariat Assistant and Office Attendant Posts

Updated: 28 November 2026

Bihar Vidhan Sabha Secretariat, Patna, has announced 20 vacancies: 15 Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) and 5 Office Attendant posts. Online applications began on 7 November 2026 and close on 6 December 2026.

Key date: Apply online by 6 December 2026. The examination date will be announced later.

Applications Open

Eligibility and Application Fee

  • JSA: Intermediate/12th pass or equivalent from a recognized board.
  • Office Attendant: Matriculation/10th pass or equivalent.
  • Age as of 1 August 2026: Minimum 18; maximum 37 for UR men, 40 for UR women and BC/EBC, and 42 for SC/ST.
  • JSA fee: ₹600 for UR/EWS/OBC/EBC men and candidates outside Bihar; ₹150 for SC/ST/PwD and Bihar domicile women.
  • Office Attendant fee: ₹400 for UR/EWS/OBC/EBC men and candidates outside Bihar; ₹100 for SC/ST/PwD and Bihar domicile women.
  • Women’s horizontal reservation is applied as specified; Bihar reservation benefits require Bihar domicile.

Selection and Pay: Point by Point

  • JSA prelims: OMR objective test, 100 questions, 400 marks, two hours; General Studies, General Science and Mathematics, Mental Ability and Reasoning.
  • JSA typing: Hindi and English typing at 30 words per minute; Hindi typing is in Kruti Dev font.
  • Office Attendant: Direct 100-mark interview; a preliminary exam may be held if applications exceed 40,000.
  • JSA pay: Level 2, ₹19,900–₹63,200.
  • Office Attendant pay: Level 1, ₹18,000–₹56,900.
