Central University of Rajasthan LDC Recruitment 2026: 2 Posts

Updated: 28 November 2026

Central University of Rajasthan (CURAJ) issued Advertisement No. 7314 on 22 October 2026 for a regular Group C Lower Division Clerk post. A 17 November addendum added one ST-reserved LDC position and extended the online application deadline to 20 December 2026.

Important: Submit the online application by 20 December 2026. The hard copy is due by 15 December 2026, so send it early and follow the official instructions.

Overview and Dates

OrganizationCentral University of Rajasthan
PostLower Division Clerk (Group C)
Vacancies2 (1 EWS and 1 ST)
AdvertisementCURAJ/R./F.179/Rectt./2026/7314; addendum dated 17 November 2026
Online application deadline20 December 2026
Hard copy deadline15 December 2026, 5:00 PM
Pay levelLevel 2

Eligibility and Pay: Point by Point

  • Two LDC seats: one EWS seat in the October advertisement and one ST seat added in the November addendum.
  • Bachelor’s degree with at least 50% marks or a master’s degree.
  • Typing speed: 35 words per minute English or 30 words per minute Hindi on a computer.
  • Knowledge of computer applications is required.
  • Maximum age is 30 years, calculated on the application deadline, subject to rules in the notice.
  • Pay level: Level 2 under the 7th Central Pay Commission, plus applicable allowances.

Fee and Application Steps

  • Fee: ₹1,500 for UR/EWS/OBC; ₹750 for SC/ST/PwBD; university regular employees are exempt.
  • Submit the online form through the official university recruitment page by 20 December 2026 (extended online deadline).
  • Print and sign the form, attach self-attested certificates and send the hard copy to the Registrar by 15 December 2026, 5:00 PM, as stated in the notice.
  • Keep payment receipt, date-of-birth proof, education records, category certificate if applicable, and application copy.

Selection Process

Applications are screened first. The university may conduct a written test, typing test, and computer test for LDC candidates, followed by document verification. Check the official recruitment page for notices and any revised schedule.
