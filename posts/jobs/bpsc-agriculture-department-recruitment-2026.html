BPSC Agriculture Department Recruitment 2026: Notification Out for 147 Assistant Director & Assistant Controller Posts

Last Updated: 28 November 2026

Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has announced 147 regular vacancies in the Agriculture Department of the Government of Bihar. Eligible candidates can apply online from 15 December 2026. Applications close on 12 January 2027, with the fee due by 11 January 2027.

Application dates: Online applications open 15 December 2026. Check the official BPSC notification for post-wise requirements and final instructions before applying.

Application Fee and Salary

  • Application fee: ₹100 per advertisement for all candidates.
  • Additional biometric fee without Aadhaar: ₹200.
  • Payment mode: Online payment methods provided on the BPSC portal.
  • Pay scale: Level 9.

Selection Process and Exam Pattern

  • Written examination: General Hindi (100 marks, qualifying), General Knowledge (100 marks), and two concerned-subject papers (200 marks each).
  • At least 30 marks are required in General Hindi; Hindi marks are not counted for merit.
  • The papers are objective. Unanswered questions carry a one-third mark deduction; candidates may use option E where provided to leave a question unattempted.
  • Category qualifying marks: UR 40%, BC 36.5%, EBC 34%, and SC/ST, women, and PwBD 32%.
  • Up to 2.5 times the vacancies may be called for interview. The personality test carries 50 marks.
  • Final exam and interview score: 550 marks. Eligible contractual experience in a similar Bihar government post may carry up to 25 preference marks.

Documents and Application Steps

  • Keep matriculation certificate, degree/provisional certificate and marksheets ready.
  • Keep category, EWS, domicile, disability, experience, and NOC documents if applicable.
  • Register through the BPSC online portal (OTR), complete profile details, upload photo and signatures, select the relevant advertisement, pay the fee, then submit the form.
  • Fee is ₹100 for each advertisement; an additional ₹200 biometric fee applies without Aadhaar.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official BPSC online application portal.
  2. Complete One Time Registration and fill in your profile.
  3. Select the relevant Agriculture Department advertisement and pay the applicable fee.
  4. Complete and submit the application form from 15 December 2026 onward.
  5. Save or print the submitted form and payment receipt.
