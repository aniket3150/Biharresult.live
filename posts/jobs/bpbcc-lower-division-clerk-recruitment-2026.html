BPBCC Lower Division Clerk Recruitment 2026: 12 Contract Posts

Updated: 28 November 2026

Bihar Police Building Construction Corporation (BPBCC) has invited offline applications for 12 contractual Lower Division Clerk posts. The one-year engagement offers a monthly remuneration of ₹28,000. Applications are accepted by post or in person through 14 December 2026, 5:30 PM.

Deadline: The completed application must reach the corporation by 14 December 2026 at 5:30 PM. No application fee is specified in the notice.

Applications Open

Overview

Organization: Bihar Police Building Construction Corporation
Post: Lower Division Clerk
Vacancies: 12
Engagement: One-year contract
Monthly remuneration: ₹28,000
Application mode: Offline

Important Dates

Notification and application start19 November 2026

Age reckoning date1 December 2026
InterviewTo be notified

Eligibility

The notice requires 10th and 12th pass with at least 60% marks in each, computer typing ability in Hindi and English, basic computer and office application knowledge, and at least three years of relevant work experience. Applicants should check the official notice for documentation and experience certificate requirements, including the organization’s CIN/GSTIN details.

Minimum age is 21 years as of 1 December 2026. The listed maximum ages are 37 years for unreserved men, 40 years for women and EBC, and 42 years for SC candidates, subject to applicable rules.

Vacancy, Fee and Selection Points

  • Category-wise vacancies: SC 2, EBC 3, EWS 1, UR 6; women’s horizontal seats are included in EBC and UR.
  • No application fee is specified.
  • The contract is for one year; monthly remuneration is ₹28,000. The notice states this engagement does not confer regular government employee status.
  • Selection steps: application screening, interview, original document verification, and final merit list.
  • Bring education, date-of-birth, domicile/category, experience, computer/typing proofs, and photographs.

How to Apply (Point by Point)

  1. Open the corporation website and find the active Lower Division Clerk contract notice.
  2. Print the prescribed form and complete all fields.
  3. Attach self-attested certificates and photographs, including experience proof with the employer CIN/GSTIN.
  4. Send by registered post or deliver in person so it reaches the corporation by 14 December 2026, 5:30 PM.

Submission address: Bihar Police Building Construction Corporation, Kautilya Nagar, Patna-14, Bihar 800014.
