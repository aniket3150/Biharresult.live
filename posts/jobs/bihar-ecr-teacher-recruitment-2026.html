East Central Railway PGT and TGT Teacher Walk-in Interviews 2026

Updated: 28 November 2026

East Central Railway (ECR), Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Division, scheduled walk-in interviews for contractual PGT and TGT teacher positions at ECR Inter College (CBSE), DDU. The notice lists interviews on 26, 27 and 28 November 2026. The number of vacancies was not specified.

Schedule date: The final listed interview date is 28 November 2026. Check the official railway notice for any revised schedule or remaining subject dates.

Interview Schedule

DateSubjects
26 November 2026PGT Commerce, PGT Chemistry, TGT Biology/Science, TGT English, TGT Mathematics/Science
27 November 2026PGT Economics, PGT Mathematics, TGT Computer, TGT Social Science/Arts
28 November 2026PGT Hindi, PGT History, TGT Hindi, TGT Hindi with Sanskrit

Application and Selection: Point by Point

  • Contractual engagement for the 2026–27 session; total vacancy count is not specified.
  • Fill the registration form before attending the walk-in interview.
  • Report by 9:00 AM on the subject’s date at ECR Inter College (CBSE), DDU, Chandauli, Uttar Pradesh 232101.
  • Selection is by interview and document verification; no written test is specified.
  • Expected qualifications: relevant postgraduate degree and B.Ed. for PGT; relevant graduate degree, B.Ed. and CTET for TGT. The short notice does not set out the full eligibility or age criteria.
  • Carry the registration printout, photo ID, school and degree certificates, B.Ed. and CTET proof where relevant, originals, and self-attested photocopies.

Registration form: Open candidate form.

Other listed subjects, including PGT Political Science, PGT English, PGT Physics, PGT Computer, TGT Urdu, TGT Library, and TGT Physical Education, had no interview dates in the schedule. Confirm any later schedule through East Central Railway’s official website.
