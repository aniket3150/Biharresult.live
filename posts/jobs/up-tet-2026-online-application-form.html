UP TET 2026 Online Application Form uptet.upessc.org

Updated On: 29 April 2026, 09:36 PM

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) invites online application for UP Teacher Eligibility Test (UP TET) 2026 for primary level class 1 to 5 and higher primary level class 6 to 8 against Advt. No. 01/ UPTET/ 2026.

All eligible citizens can submit online application form on the official UPESSC portal uptet.upessc.org from 27 March 2026 to 03 May 2026.

UP TET Eligibility Criteria 2026

Class 1 to 5Class 6 to 8
Senior Secondary with 50% marks and 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (passed/appearing). Graduation and 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (passed/appearing).
Senior Secondary with 45% marks and 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education as per NCTE norms. Graduation with 50% marks and 1-year B.Ed (passed/appearing).
Graduation with 50% marks and B.Ed (plus mandatory bridge course after appointment as per rules). Graduation with 50% marks and 1-year B.Ed Special Education (passed/appearing).

List of Required Documents for UP TET 2026 Application

  1. Passport Size Photograph: 30 KB - 300 KB.
  2. Signature: 10 KB - 300 KB.
  3. Documents and Declaration: 50 KB - 500 KB.
  4. Valid Photo Identity Proof in PDF format.
  5. 10th, 12th, Graduation and other required certificates in PDF.
