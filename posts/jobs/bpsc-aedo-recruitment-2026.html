BPSC AEDO Recruitment 2026: 935 Assistant Education Development Officer Posts

Updated: 28 November 2026

Bihar Public Service Commission issued fresh Advertisement No. 16/2026 on 26 November 2026 for 935 Assistant Education Development Officer (AEDO) vacancies in the Education Department. The earlier Advertisement No. 87/2025 has been cancelled. Candidates must apply under the fresh advertisement.

Applications open 1 December 2026. The fee payment deadline is 30 December and the application deadline is 31 December 2026.

Eligibility, Pay and Fee

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent from a recognized university.
  • Age: Minimum 21 years; category maximums and relaxations are in Advertisement 16/2026.
  • Pay: Level 5, initial basic pay ₹29,200.
  • Fee: ₹100; additional biometric fee applies without Aadhaar as described in the notice.
  • Reservation: 935 posts include 319 horizontal women’s reservation posts; reservation follows Bihar domicile rules.

Selection Process

  • Preliminary examination is a screening test. Category qualifying marks are specified in the notice.
  • Up to ten times the vacancies may be shortlisted for the main examination.
  • The main examination has General Language (qualifying), General Studies, and General Aptitude papers.
  • Final merit is prepared from main examination marks, followed by document checks.

How to Apply

  1. Read fresh Advertisement 16/2026 and its online application instructions.
  2. Register/sign in on the BPSC online portal from 1 December 2026.
  3. Complete the form and pay the fee by 30 December 2026.
  4. Submit the application by 31 December 2026 and save the submitted form and receipt.

The fresh notice replaces cancelled Advertisement 87/2025. Applicants must submit a new form under Advertisement 16/2026.
