Eligibility Details

72nd Combined (Preliminary) Competitive ExaminationCandidates must have passed Graduation (Bachelor's Degree) or an equivalent examination from a recognized university.
Child Development Project OfficerCandidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university and must choose one optional subject for the Main Examination from: Home Science, Psychology, Sociology, Labour & Social Welfare.
Financial Administrative Officer & EquivalentCandidates must have a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university or its affiliated institution in any one subject among Commerce, Economics, Mathematics, or Statistics.
Sub-Divisional Protection OfficerCandidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university in either Psychology or Law. Additionally, candidates must choose one optional subject for the Main Examination from Psychology or Law.
