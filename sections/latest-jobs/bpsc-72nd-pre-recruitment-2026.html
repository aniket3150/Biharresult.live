BPSC 72nd Pre Recruitment 2026

Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released a Notification on its official website for the 72nd Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) Pre 2026. This recruitment is for 1186 posts. The BPSC application form has started on 07 May 2026, and candidates can apply until 31 May 2026. The minimum age required is 20 years, and the maximum age limit is 37 - 42 years (Category Wise) as of 01 August 2026.
