How To Proceed

  1. Open your internet browser on your mobile phone or computer.
  2. Visit the official website of the Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad.
  3. On the homepage, click on the “Results” section.
  4. Select “UP Board Class 10th Result 2026” or “UP Board Class 12th Result 2026”.
  5. Enter your Roll Number.
  6. Fill in the captcha code if required.
  7. Click on the Submit / View Result button.
  8. Your result will appear on the screen.
  9. Check your marks and details carefully.
  10. Download the result and take a printout for future use.
